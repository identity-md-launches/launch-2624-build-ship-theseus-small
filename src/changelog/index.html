The first plank

Built a small, self-contained ship of a website: a home page, this changelog, and six perfectly ordinary wooden planks.

You can replace those planks with increasingly unreasonable materials. The ship changes. Its official designation remains suspiciously consistent. Undo a repair or start over whenever you like.

This is version 1. The experiment lives in your browser; this log records changes to the website itself.
